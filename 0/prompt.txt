Product positioning
Build Founder Radar as a global founder intelligence platform.
Product promise: Discover founders, understand their professional journeys, investigate relevant signals, and organize research in one workspace.
The platform should support a broad range of founder research use cases across countries, industries, company stages, and professional backgrounds. Do not optimize the entire product for VCs, sales teams, one geography, or one industry.
1. Core product workflow
A. Discover founders
Build a polished discovery experience using the existing Crustdata People Search API.
Support available filters such as:
Current or previous employer.
Current or previous role.
Location and country.
Professional background.
Career history and relevant transitions.
Recent job changes, where supported.
Other useful filters supported by the configured API.
Include a natural-language search entry point, filter controls, saved searches, pagination where supported, and clear loading, error, and empty states.
Natural-language searches must be translated into supported API queries or transparent post-processing. Do not claim that unsupported filters work.
B. Founder intelligence dossiers
Each profile must have a detailed research view containing:
Professional profile information.
Career timeline based on available employment history.
Current and previous roles and employers.
Relevant founder indicators.
Source links and evidence where available.
AI-generated research summary.
Explanation of why the profile matches the search.
Missing information and suggested verification steps.
Editable research notes.
Save-to-watchlist action.
Outreach draft generation.
Clearly separate source-backed facts from AI-generated interpretations.
C. Career signals and founder discovery intelligence
Identify potentially relevant founder indicators and career transitions using available profile data.
Support useful discovery patterns such as:
People with previous experience at a target company who now hold founder roles.
People with recent job changes and relevant founder indicators.
People whose available professional profiles indicate a possible new venture.
Founders in particular locations, industries, or professional domains.
Use actual employment dates, titles, and other available evidence.
Classify important conclusions transparently as verified, unverified, or insufficient evidence. These classifications must follow explicit rules based on the available data.
Never infer that someone is launching a startup solely because they left an employer or have a missing current role.
D. Saved founders and watchlists
Implement persistent storage for:
Saved founders.
User-created watchlists.
Research notes.
Recent searches.
Relevant profile metadata.
Users should be able to save, remove, organize, and revisit profiles.
Do not implement automatic monitoring or claim that a profile has changed unless the application has genuinely retrieved and compared updated data.
E. AI research and outreach
Use Claude through the existing LiteLLM gateway for:
Founder research summaries.
Search relevance explanations.
Career-history synthesis.
Missing-evidence identification.
Suggested follow-up research questions.
Personalized outreach drafts.
Use reusable prompts, sensible timeouts, output validation, and graceful error handling.
Keep generated outreach editable. Do not automatically send external communications.
